Underlying sources: Z-Anatomy (https://www.z-anatomy.com/) and BodyParts3D, copyright The Database Center for Life Science (https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/). Some deep structures have additional atlas sources detailed in the source license.

SHA256: `76a49ea4526a4880613aec7a02756bd7301b0b9d0680d7cae33e197b672c5453`.

The anatomical surface is educational context fitted inside the acoustic phantom, not a registered patient-specific acoustic map. Physics uses the rendered ellipsoidal medium boundaries.
